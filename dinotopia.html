Dinotopia

A cozy island game about raising your own dinosaurs. Hatch eggs, feed your dinos as they grow, and build a life on the island — while your grown companions gather resources alongside you.

🎮 Playable in your browser 📱 Mobile version in progress 🧡 Cozy · family friendly

The game

Raise them well.

Every dinosaur on your island starts as an egg. What happens next is up to you — what you feed them, where they live, and what kind of island you build together.

Hatch & raise

Care for your dinos through every life stage — from wobbly hatchling to towering companion.

Build your island

Turn a quiet stretch of sand into a home. Everything on your island is earned, not bought.

Gather together

Grown dinos pitch in — gathering resources alongside you while you plan what's next.

Cozy pace

No timers yelling at you. Dinotopia is happy to see you whether you play a little or a lot.

Playtesting

Help shape Dinotopia.

Dinotopia is in active playtesting. If you're playing and something feels off — or something feels great — we genuinely want to hear about it.
