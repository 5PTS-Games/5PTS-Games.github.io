Hakai

Hit the huge ramps at full throttle, fly further than your last run, and find out how much a car can take. Every car is built on soft-body physics, so no two crashes are the same.

The game

Hakai means "destruction".

Panels fold where they are hit, doors and hoods swing on their hinges and tear away, windows crack and then shatter, paint scrapes off, and a car pushed too far goes up in flames.

Huge ramps

Eleven levels, each with its own way to fly and its own way to crash: drops, wall rides, a giant pipe, a skate park and an endless bobsled.

Real car damage

A nose into a wall crumples the front, a roll flattens the roof. One crash too many and the car explodes.

Nine cars

Start in the Volkov and work up through trucks, muscle cars and supercars. Upgrade speed, durability and income.

Make it yours

Paints, camouflage and animated skins: fire, lightning and storms that move across the bodywork.
