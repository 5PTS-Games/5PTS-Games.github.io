Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026 · Applies to the game “Dinotopia” by 5PTS Games

The short version

Dinotopia does not collect, store, or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics trackers we run, and no ads served by us.

Game saves

Your game progress (inventory, dinosaurs, settings) is saved locally on your device, or — when playing on a platform such as Facebook Instant Games — in that platform's own save storage attached to your platform account. We do not operate servers of our own and cannot see your save data.

Platforms

When you play Dinotopia inside a platform (for example Facebook Instant Games or a web game portal), that platform may process data as described in its own privacy policy — for Facebook, see the Meta Privacy Policy. We only receive what the platform provides to run the game (such as a loading context), and we do not use it to identify you.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever adds features that handle personal data (such as online features or ads), this policy will be updated first, with the date above.
