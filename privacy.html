Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026 · 5PTS Games LLC

The short version: we collect as little as we can. Our games may use third-party services for ads, analytics, and in-app purchases, and those services process some device data (like an advertising identifier) as described below. We don't sell your personal information, we don't run accounts, and our website sets no tracking cookies.

1. Who we are

This policy is provided by 5PTS Games LLC ("5PTS Games", "we", "us"), an independent game studio. It applies to our games — Dinotopia, Titan Miners, and Hookshot Hero — and to our website, 5ptsgames.com.

Questions or requests about this policy: contact@5ptsgames.com.

2. What we collect, and why

Information you give us

If you email us (support, feedback, playtesting), we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem you wrote about.

Information collected automatically in our games

Depending on the game and platform, our games may use third-party services for advertising, analytics, and in-app purchases. Through those services, the following kinds of data may be collected automatically:

  • Device information — device model, operating system version, language, and general (city-level) location inferred from your IP address.
  • Identifiers — your device's resettable advertising identifier (Apple IDFA / Google Advertising ID) and app-level identifiers.
  • Gameplay and diagnostics — anonymous events such as levels played, sessions, crashes, and performance data, used to find bugs and improve the games.
  • Purchase information — if you make an in-app purchase, the app store processes the payment. We receive a record of the transaction (what was bought and when) but never your card or bank details.

Our website

5ptsgames.com is a static site. We set no cookies and run no analytics or ad trackers on it. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log basic request data (such as IP addresses) for security — see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives the request for the font files — see the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

3. Advertising

Some of our games show ads from third-party advertising partners. Those partners may use your device's advertising identifier and related device data to serve and measure ads, including — where you have consented — personalized ads.

You are always in control of ad personalization:

  • iOS: our games ask permission before tracking via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • In the EU/UK: where required, we show a consent prompt before any personalized ads are served.

4. Analytics

We use analytics to understand how our games are played — which levels are too hard, where crashes happen, what players enjoy. This data is aggregated and used only to improve the games. We do not use it to identify individual players.

5. In-app purchases

All purchases in our mobile games are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Refunds are handled by the store where the purchase was made — see our support page for how to request one.

6. How we share information

We share data only with:

  • Service providers — the advertising, analytics, and platform services described above, which process data to provide their service to us;
  • Legal authorities — if required by law or to protect our rights or players' safety;
  • A successor — if 5PTS Games is ever acquired or merged, players would be notified of any change to this policy.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

7. Game saves

Your game progress is saved on your device, or — when playing a browser version of one of our games on a platform such as a web game portal — in that platform's own save storage attached to your platform account. We do not operate account servers of our own and cannot see your save data.

8. Children

Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where our games use advertising or analytics, we configure those services in line with the platform's family and children's-data policies. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at contact@5ptsgames.com and we will delete it.

9. Data retention

Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve the issue and for a reasonable period afterwards. Data held by our third-party services is retained according to their own policies; analytics data we control is kept in aggregate form only.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/UK under GDPR and California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to opt out of targeted advertising. To exercise any of these rights, email contact@5ptsgames.com — we honor requests regardless of where you live, and we will never treat you differently for making one.

11. Security

We minimize what we collect in the first place, use reputable platform services, and restrict access to any personal data (like support emails) to the small team that needs it.

12. International transfers

We are based in the United States, and the services our games use may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses implemented by our service providers.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy — for example when a game adds new features that handle personal data — we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. Meaningful changes will also be noted in the games' store listings where applicable.
