Hookshot Henry — Data Deletion Request

Hookshot Henry, published by 5PTS Games LLC, does not require an account and does not store your personal data on our own servers. The only data associated with your use of the app is advertising and analytics data collected by our ad partners — Google AdMob and Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) — tied to your device's advertising identifier.

Delete or dissociate your data

  1. Reset or delete your Advertising ID. On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads and choose "Delete advertising ID" (or "Reset advertising ID"). This stops further ad-ID-based collection and dissociates prior data.
  2. Withdraw ad consent. In Hookshot Henry, open the menu and choose "Privacy options" (shown where applicable) to change or withdraw your advertising consent.
  3. Remove on-device data. Uninstalling the app — or clearing its data in Settings → Apps → Hookshot Henry → Storage — removes all data stored on your device (progress, coins, and settings).

Request deletion by email

To request that our ad partners delete data associated with your device, or for any other data request, email contact@5ptsgames.com with the subject Data Deletion Request. We will process your request and, where applicable, forward it to our ad partners.
