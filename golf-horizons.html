Golf Horizons

One-shot golf on a course that never ends. Pull back, let go, and try for a hole in one. Every nine holes takes you somewhere new: meadows, dunes, rocky shores, islands and pine woods.

The game

There's always another horizon.

Every hole is made fresh, and the course starts gentle and slowly gets harder the further you travel. Sink it in as few shots as you can.

One shot, one hole

Pull back from anywhere to aim, let go to shoot, and pull at an angle to curve it in.

A course that never ends

New holes forever, across five worlds, four seasons and every time of day.

Real golf scoring

Every hole has a par. Birdies, eagles and holes in one earn the most points.

Twelve ball trails

Spend your points on trails from chalk and comet to plasma and neon, and recolour them.
