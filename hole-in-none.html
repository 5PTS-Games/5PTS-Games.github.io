Hole In None

You're the golf hole, and every golfer on the range is aiming at you. Slide across the grass, dodge every ball, and hold out until the range closes for the night.

Play in your browser
🌐 Free in your browser 📱 Phone, tablet & desktop ⛳ No ads. Everything free.

The game

Every ball has your name on it.

Each hole is one day at the range, from morning to pitch-black night. It starts with a few golfers and builds into a storm of balls. Anything that drops in ends the run.

You are the hole

Drag anywhere or use WASD to slide around the range. Don't let a single ball drop in.

Eight kinds of trouble

Heat-seekers, rollers, giants, orbital drops, and tar and ice that leave sticky or slippery patches.

The golf-cart shop

The range picker collects the balls you dodge. Spend them between holes on a smaller hole, a gopher, an umbrella and more.

18 holes, then endless

Every hole is longer and busier than the last. Clear all 18, then keep going for as long as you last.

Tee off

No download. Just play.

Hole In None runs in any modern browser, on phone or desktop, in portrait or landscape. Your run and best score save on your device.
