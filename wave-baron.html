Wave Baron: Pirate Fleet

Command a growing pirate fleet against bigger and bigger armadas. Plunder every wave for profit, commandeer the ships you beat, and see how long your fleet can rule the sea.

🌐 Coming to your browser 🏴‍☠️ Plunder · commandeer · repeat

The game

Every wave is bigger. So is your fleet.

Wave Baron is built around one loop: beat the armada in front of you, take its gold and its ships, and sail into an even bigger one.

Command a fleet

Lead a whole fleet into battle, not a single ship — and watch it grow.

Endless waves

The armadas keep coming, and every one is bigger than the last. How long can you hold the sea?

Plunder for profit

Every armada you beat pays out. Spend the haul on a stronger fleet.

Commandeer their ships

Beat a ship and it sails for you. Turn their armada into your own.

Stay in the loop

Setting sail soon.
