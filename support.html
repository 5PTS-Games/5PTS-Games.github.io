FAQ

Common questions.

How do I report a bug?

Email contact@5ptsgames.com with the game's name in the subject line. The most helpful reports include: what you were doing when it happened, what you expected, what actually happened, and your device or browser. A screenshot or screen recording is gold.

How do refunds for in-app purchases work?

Purchases made through an app store are processed and refunded by that store, not by us:

  • Google Play: request a refund through the Google Play refund process.
  • Apple App Store: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

If a purchase didn't arrive in-game after a successful payment, email us and we'll make it right.

Where is my save data stored? Can you restore it?

Game progress is saved on your device (or in the hosting platform's storage for browser versions of our games). Because we don't run our own account servers, we generally can't restore progress from a deleted or reset device — but email us and we'll do what we can.

How do I join a playtest?

Email us with the name of the game you'd like to test in the subject line. Dinotopia is playtesting now; Titan Miners and Hookshot Hero playtests will open as they get closer to release.

How do you handle my data?

The short version: we collect as little as possible, and we spell all of it out in our privacy policy. For data questions or deletion requests, email contact@5ptsgames.com.

I have a business or press inquiry — who do I talk to?

Same inbox, same humans: contact@5ptsgames.com. Put "business" or "press" in the subject and it'll find the right person.

Still stuck?

Email us directly — a real person will get back to you.
