The studio

We're 5PTS Games.

An independent game studio making mobile and web games. Small team, no publisher, no outside noise — just games we'd want to play ourselves, built until they're right.

What we believe.

Feel first. Every 5PTS game starts with one feeling we want you to have — cozy, greedy, weightless — and everything else gets built in service of it.

Respect the player. Games should be happy to see you whether you play for two minutes or two hours. No dark patterns, no guilt trips.

Ship small, ship sharp. Being a small studio is a feature. We'd rather ship a tight game that does one thing brilliantly than a bloated one that does ten things badly.

We're currently building three games: Dinotopia, a cozy island game about raising dinosaurs; Titan Miners, a mining game with titan-sized payoffs; and Hookshot Hero, an action game built on momentum.
